This is a project made with Power BI and it’s about the sales of a multi-national grocery chain with locations in Canada, Mexico and the United States. 
For this, I cleaned the data, filled in missing values and built a relational model by connecting the tables. 
Then I designed a creative dashboard that presents Current Month’s Transactions and Curren Month’s Profit to have risen to 5,5%. 
Additionally, the weekly revenue trending and revenue vs. target are metrics represented by charts. 
Lastly, a matrix that shows the Total Transactions, Total Profit, Profit Margin and Return Rate of a product.
